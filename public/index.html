Pay is worked out on the day's rate for the whole shift span. Overtime is always paid at exactly the hours worked, at that rate multiplied by the OT multiplier — leave it at 1 if overtime pays the same. If the ordinary (non-overtime) time on a day works out under the minimum, it's paid up to it; set the minimum to 0 to turn that off. A day with a duty numbered RL uses the RL minimum instead of the standard one.
